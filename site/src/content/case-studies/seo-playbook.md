## Problem
Every SEO project started with the same questions and a new spreadsheet, so the quality depended on the day.

## Approach
A 9-phase playbook from kick-off to monthly reporting, 9 templates (kick-off questionnaire, keyword research, site architecture, content brief, content calendar, internal-linking map, on-page checklist, monthly report) and a Python validator that checks every filled-in CSV: column names, dropdown values and the priority score formula. My own content site (S2) is the worked example: 37 keywords, 37 briefs, a 19-week calendar — all passing the validator.
